# Entry 6 — Humans Name Things After Humans.

Status: full draft approved; preserved as `06-humans-name-things-after-humans.md`

## Scope

Eponyms turn discovery, prestige, affection, patronage, power, and memory into linguistic territory. The practice can preserve contributors, obscure other contributors, reward patrons, reproduce historical power, or allow an individual human to remain embedded in a unit, organism, disease, mountain, law, or phenomenon.

Maintain Leth's fondness without ignoring that naming can also be a claim of ownership. This entry should complicate the Human Spark thesis: not every residue was left innocently, and not every human involved in a discovery was equally permitted to leave a name.

Classification: **Memorial Behaviors → Linguistic Territory → Eponymy**
