# Entry 5 — It Resembled a Horse from One Direction.

Status: approved title and scope; full draft pending

## Scope

Humans understand unfamiliar organisms by naming them after familiar ones, often on the basis of one feature, one angle, or one observer's momentary impression. Explore names such as seahorse, starfish, jellyfish, guinea pig, prairie dog, and other creatures whose common names suggest relationships they do not possess.

The humor should arise from Leth attempting to infer biological relationships from common names before learning that resemblance frequently outranks taxonomy in human language.

Classification: **Comparative Naming → Visual Resemblance → Questionable Kinship**
