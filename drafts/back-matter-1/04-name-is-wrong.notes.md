# Entry 4 — The Name Is Wrong. It Has Seniority.

Status: approved concept; full draft pending

## Required primary example: Pluto

Humans did not receive Pluto's reclassification as an improvement in taxonomy. They responded as though Pluto had been personally punished. “Demoted” is a social description, transforming a scientific category change into something done *to Pluto*.

The scientific category changed. The human relationship did not. Pluto had already been taught to children, placed in songs and mnemonics, incorporated into models, and assigned the emotional role of the small, cold, distant member of the solar family. The revised classification arrived with superior evidence. The earlier classification had seniority.

## Approved passages

> Humans will continue using a name after discovering that it is inaccurate.
>
> This is not always caused by ignorance.
>
> Sometimes the name arrived first, remained long enough to acquire familiarity, and can no longer be removed without the humans experiencing the correction as a minor bereavement.

> When astronomers later reclassified Pluto as a dwarf planet, the public did not receive this as an improvement in taxonomy.
>
> They received it as an injustice.
>
> Humans said Pluto had been *demoted.*
>
> Several demanded that it be restored.
>
> Pluto itself continued orbiting the Sun and appears not to have filed an appeal.

Classification: **Corrected Knowledge → Emotional Seniority → Terminological Persistence**
